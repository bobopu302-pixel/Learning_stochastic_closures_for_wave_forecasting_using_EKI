  statistics (2 variances, 8 autocorrelation lags, 18 signed cross-correlations, 10 band
  energies); model M = 10 modes, dim theta = 34 = 2(R+3)+M with R = 9 nodes.
- **C4.2 Calibration settings**: T_y = T_G = 1000 s, N_G = 100 (shared random numbers within an
  iteration), N_Gamma = 200, Gamma diagonal, J = 100, 30 iterations (cap); priors
  sqrt(sigma_m)~U(0.5,6.0), tau~U(0.05,1.0), a~U(0.5,8.0), ell_q~U(0.75,3.0), ell_p~U(2.0,10.0).
- **C4.3 Learned closure slopes**: slopes at the origin -0.049 (Phi_q) and -0.286 (Phi_p), sum
  -0.335 = shared linear damping rate of every mode.
- **C4.4 Objective descent**: from 1.4e10 at the prior to 80.6 (= 4.2 q/2); over last ten
  iterations moves between 81 and 115 (~9% scatter); residual split by family: 56%
  cross-correlations, 35% band energies, 9% autocorrelation, variances ~0.
- **C4.5 Spectrum/energy**: elevation-spectrum L1 error 6.9%; total band energy -2.0%; noise
  amplitudes sqrt(sigma_m) rise from 0.17 to 1.05 and fall back to 0.37, peaking at the sixth
  mode (one grid step above the 1.0 Hz spectral peak).
- **C4.6 Long-run distribution**: Var(eta) 1.109 vs 0.995; H_s 4.213 m vs 3.989 m (+5.6%,
  variance +11.5%); excess kurtosis +0.362 vs -0.028 (reference Gaussian).
- **C4.7 Spatial structure**: 17 of 18 cross-correlation components low by up to 1.5 percentage
  points; worst single component -6.7 sd; cause = single shared pair of functions -> same damping
  rate for all modes.
- **C4.8 Beyond fitted lags**: reference decorrelates within 2 s; model autocorrelation envelope
  ~ +/-0.75 out to 20 s with recurrences at 6, 12, 18 s (beat period 1/Delta_f = 6 s for
  Delta_f = 0.167 Hz); raising M to 25 moves first recurrence to 16 s and lowers its height from
  0.82 to 0.42 (both M=10 curves peak 0.82 at 6.2 s).
- **C4.9 Appendix A.6 supporting numbers (settings rationale)**: reference-side variances span
  7.9 orders of magnitude (2.8e-8 to 2.1) -> diagonal Gamma; ramp prior (independent uniforms
  would put only ~38% of mass in the dissipative region); stationarity gate (tr A < 0, det A > 0)
  fired 45 times in the first four iterations (10, 24, 10, 1) and never afterwards; objective
  descends nine orders of magnitude in first twelve iterations, then 81-115 over last ten.
- **C4.10 Appendix A.5 reference amplitudes**: Gaussian envelope f_p = 1.0 Hz, Delta_f = 0.35 Hz,
  scaled so total elevation variance sum a_n^2/2 = 1 m^2; frequencies drawn once from a fixed
  seed.
